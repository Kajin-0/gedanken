# Gedanken

A repository for first-principles thought experiments aimed at exposing sharp conceptual tensions between established physical theories.

The method is deliberately simple:

1. isolate one physical principle;
2. remove every unnecessary experimental detail;
3. force two accepted descriptions to make predictions about the same idealized situation;
4. identify the smallest observable on which those predictions differ;
5. distinguish established results from genuinely open questions;
6. only then ask whether the thought experiment suggests a practical experiment or a publishable theoretical result.

The goal is not speculative model-building for its own sake. The goal is to find situations in which nature is forced to answer a precise question.

## Experiments

### 01 — Causal Transport of Quantum Branch Information by Gravity

**Question:** If a mass is placed in a coherent spatial superposition, can gravity communicate information about the alternatives to a distant quantum system without first converting those alternatives into classical information?

The experiment develops from a single spatially superposed source, through gravity-mediated which-path information and reversible entanglement, to the relativistic question of whether that quantum information propagates causally through the gravitational field.

## Status

These are working theoretical notes, not claims of discovery. Each experiment should eventually contain:

- the minimal Gedanken experiment;
- the mathematical model;
- competing hypotheses and observables;
- known literature and novelty boundary;
- experimental scaling;
- unresolved conceptual loopholes;
- a compressed paper-level statement of the result.
