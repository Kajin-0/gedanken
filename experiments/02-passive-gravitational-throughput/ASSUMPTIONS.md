# Working Assumptions — Experiment 02

These assumptions define the **initial test class**, not a final theorem. They may be weakened, sharpened, or rejected as the derivation develops.

## Included initially

- weak linearized gravity on an approximately flat background;
- nonrelativistic compact matter at each endpoint;
- leading mass-quadrupole coupling;
- source and receiver separated far enough for a propagating-wave treatment;
- passive, linear, time-invariant endpoint dynamics;
- finite or countably infinite modal sectors when the relevant port operators are bounded/admissible;
- narrowband analysis when a single representative angular frequency `omega` is used outside an integral;
- arbitrary passive internal mode mixing unless a derivation requires more structure;
- all unobserved loss channels retained in the passive model rather than silently discarded.

## Not assumed without proof

The following must not be inserted merely because they make the candidate theorem convenient:

- single-mode endpoints;
- equal source and receiver linewidths;
- critical coupling;
- orthogonal radiation patterns;
- identical source/receiver geometries;
- high-`Q` or low-`Q` limits;
- exact reciprocity beyond what the physical model actually supplies;
- saturation of any intermediate inequality;
- a finite resonance count.

## Initially excluded

These regimes are outside the first reconstruction and must not be claimed as covered:

- active gain, inversion, parametric pumping, or externally powered feedback that changes the passive energy balance;
- extended phased apertures whose size is not compact relative to the wavelength;
- added gravitational relays, mirrors, or externally engineered cavities;
- reactive/near-field exchange where a separated propagating-wave approximation fails;
- relativistic or strongly nonlinear matter motion;
- higher-multipole dominance;
- strong-field or curved-background focusing effects;
- arbitrary unbounded PDE boundary-control ports without a separate admissibility analysis;
- genuinely non-Markov continua unless explicitly treated;
- quantum-gravity corrections beyond quantization of the linearized field/matter modes.

## Asymptotic discipline

Every use of a leading wave-zone or narrowband expression must state the retained order. Equality must not be used where only an upper-bound asymptotic has been shown.

In particular, repeated scattering or recurrence must be analyzed as an inequality before any claim is made that it preserves a leading coefficient.
