\title{An Inertia-Controlled Spectral-Area Bound for Passive Far-Zone Gravitational Transduction}

\author{Terence Fisher}
\email{terence@brooks-photonics.com}
\affiliation{Brooks Photonics}

\date{August 2026}

\begin{abstract}
We derive a frequency-integrated upper bound on coherent transfer between two separated compact passive matter systems coupled through propagating linearized gravity. For a narrowband complex-envelope transfer matrix $T(\nu)$, define
\begin{equation}
\Gamma_{\rm coh}=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathcal B_\nu}\dd\nu\,\Tr[T^\dagger(\nu)T(\nu)].
\end{equation}
For energy-normalized ports this is the band-limited $H_2$ spectral area: the integral of power transmissivity over detuning. It therefore measures cumulative transfer strength across the operating band rather than peak resonant height, and it is not an information capacity or bit rate. Passivity bounds this spectral area by the smaller total gravitational coupling trace of the two endpoints. For compact nonrelativistic linear-harmonic matter, mass-weighted modal completeness bounds the retained cumulative quadrupole resource by the scalar second mass moment $I_2=\int\rho r^2\dd^3x$. Normalized transverse-traceless quadrupole radiation has maximum directivity $5/2$, giving the leading separated-wave-zone propagation ceiling $25/[16(k_0R)^2]$. Combining these results yields
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\Gamma_{\rm coh}
\lesssim
\frac{25G\omega_0^2}{12c^3R^2}\min(I_{2,A},I_{2,B})
}
\end{equation}
for carrier frequency $\omega_0$, envelope bandwidth $B\ll\omega_0$, compact endpoints with $k_0a_A,k_0a_B\ll1$, separation $k_0R\gg1$, and a finite or countably infinite bounded-port Markov modal sector retained at the carrier scale. Higher-frequency off-resonant sectors require separate control and are not included in the simple carrier-scale resource. Passive internal mode mixing and repeated passive returns between the same two compact endpoints do not change the retained leading coefficient. The result is a two-ended passive spectral-area ceiling controlled only by endpoint mass distribution and far-zone TT propagation within this explicitly restricted class.
\end{abstract}
