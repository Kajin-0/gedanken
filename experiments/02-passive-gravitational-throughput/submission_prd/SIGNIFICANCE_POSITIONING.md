# Significance Positioning — PRD

## Strongest defensible framing

Experiment 02 is a **conceptual passive-resource/no-go theorem** for propagating linearized gravity. Its value is not that it predicts an accessible signal level, but that it removes a large class of passive engineering degrees of freedom from the leading frequency-integrated ceiling.

Within the theorem class, high quality factor, additional retained resonances, passive mode hybridization, endpoint asymmetry, and same-two-endpoint recurrence can reshape spectral transfer but cannot raise the cumulative ceiling beyond

```math
\Gamma_{\rm coh}
\lesssim
\frac{25G\omega_0^2}{12c^3R^2}\min(I_{2,A},I_{2,B}).
```

That is the editorial story.

## Practical limitation to state plainly

For macroscopic mechanical frequencies, the wave-zone condition `k_0R >> 1` can require very large endpoint separations. In that regime gravitational coupling is extraordinarily weak. The paper should therefore avoid implying near-term experimental utility or detector relevance.

This limitation does **not** invalidate the theorem; it changes the significance claim from experimental to conceptual.

## Important metric discipline

`Gamma_coh` has units `s^-1`, but it is a frequency-integrated coherent-transfer spectral area. It is **not** an information rate. Therefore statements such as “one bit would take `1/Gamma_coh` seconds” are not justified without a separate signaling/noise/capacity model.

Likewise, do not compare `Gamma_coh` directly to a strain-noise PSD.

## Dominant referee risk

The most likely substantive publication objection is significance:

> Once historical resonant-mass response, arbitrary-body modal theory, material sum rules, generic passive `H2`, and wave propagation bounds are credited, is the residual complete two-ended closure sufficiently nontrivial to merit publication?

The response should not inflate priority. It should emphasize what disappears from the final theorem: the passive internal architecture of **both** endpoints, reduced to a single scalar second-moment resource and a propagation cut.

## What not to do

- Do not sell the theorem as a practical gravitational-wave detector limit.
- Do not sell it as a quantum-information capacity theorem.
- Do not claim that gravity-as-communication is new.
- Do not hide the severe weak-coupling scale.
- Do not broaden beyond compact, narrowband, wave-zone, retained carrier-scale bounded-port assumptions.

## What to emphasize

The result is a clean cut-set/resource law for a physically specific interaction: within the declared class, passive resonant engineering cannot manufacture cumulative gravitational oscillator strength or overcome the smaller endpoint resource.
