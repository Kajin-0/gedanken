## Rule for closing an objection

Do not mark an objection resolved merely because an alternative derivation reproduces the existing answer. The objection is closed only when the reviewer's **specific failure mode** has been reproduced and shown either

1. not to occur;
2. to lie outside the declared theorem class; or
3. to require a correction that has been incorporated and revalidated.
