# Experiment 02 — External Specialist Review Package

## Purpose

This package is designed for **adversarial external review**, not promotional circulation.

The manuscript has reached an internal stopping point. The dominant remaining risks are:

1. an older gravitational-antenna / network theorem equivalent to the final two-ended inertia closure;
2. a hidden failure in the passive selected-port / gravitational-continuum representation; or
3. the possibility that the exact closure is technically correct but insufficiently significant relative to its established ingredients.

The reviewer is therefore asked to try to **break the result**, not to improve its presentation.

For separated compact passive nonrelativistic linear-harmonic source and receiver systems in weak quadrupolar wave-zone gravity,

```math
\Gamma_{\rm coh}
=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\mathcal B}
```


The claim is **not** that the ingredients are new. The candidate contribution is the complete gravity-specific two-ended elimination of device-specific passive parameters in favor of endpoint inertia and free-space separation.

## Review protocol

Do **not** ask a reviewer whether the paper is "interesting" in the abstract. Ask for a falsifiable judgment on one of three things:

- **Correctness:** identify a counterexample, normalization defect, invalid operator step, or violated physical assumption.
- **Priority:** identify a primary source that already states the same inertia-closed two-ended theorem, even in different notation.
- **Significance:** explain why the final parameter elimination adds no substantive physical information beyond established gravitational antenna / passive-wave results.

A useful external response is therefore either

```text
A. I found a technical failure. Here it is.
B. I found an equivalent prior theorem. Here is the source/equation.
C. I do not see either A or B, but the synthesis is too incremental because ...
D. I do not see A or B, and I regard the closed theorem as a legitimate specialist contribution.
```

## Internal freeze rule

Do not broaden the theorem while external review is pending. Only reopen derivation if a reviewer supplies a concrete technical or priority objection.
