## What counts as a successful review

A response of only "looks interesting" is not useful. The objective is to obtain one of:

- a concrete counterexample;
- a specific invalid equation or normalization;
- a primary source containing the equivalent theorem;
- a technically argued judgment that the closure is too immediate to publish; or
- a statement that the targeted specialist could not find such a defect in their domain.
