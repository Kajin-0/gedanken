## Journals not recommended as the first submission

### Physical Review Letters

Do not compress the present manuscript into PRL merely for prestige. The theorem has several load-bearing scope conditions—compactness, wave-zone asymptotics, narrowband carrier normalization, retained modal sector, bounded-port assumptions, and recurrence limitations. Aggressive Letter compression would increase the probability that a referee interprets the result as overbroad or underproved. A PRL attempt would make more sense only after external specialists independently establish that the result has unusually broad significance.

### Detector/instrumentation journals

The paper is not a gravitational-wave detector sensitivity calculation and should not be sent primarily as instrumentation work. `Gamma_coh` is a coherent-transfer spectral area with units `s^-1`, not a strain-noise PSD.

### Quantum-information journals

Recent work already treats gravity as a communication mediator. The candidate contribution here is not a new information-theory theorem; it is the passive far-zone TT two-ended inertia closure. A quantum-information venue would invite the wrong comparison class and obscure the actual result.
