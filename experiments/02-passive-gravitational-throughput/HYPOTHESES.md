# Hypotheses — Experiment 02

Every item in this file is provisional until promoted in `CLAIM_LEDGER.md` with derivation and validation evidence.

## H0 — null hypothesis

There is no useful universal inertia-only ceiling for the stated passive class; additional material, modal, or port parameters remain irreducible.

This hypothesis must remain live throughout the project.

## H1 — passive selected-port spectral cut

For an appropriate passive realization of the endpoint dynamics, the integrated selected-port transfer may admit a bound of schematic form

```math
\Gamma_{\rm coh}
\le
\eta_{\max}
\min\!\left[
\mathcal R_A,\mathcal R_B
\right],
```

where `mathcal R_A` and `mathcal R_B` are total gravitational coupling resources at the two endpoints.

Open issues:

- exact normalization of `Gamma_coh`;
- sufficient realization assumptions;
- finite versus countably infinite sectors;
- treatment of unbounded or continuum ports.

## H2 — cumulative endpoint resource is inertia controlled

For compact nonrelativistic quadrupolar matter, the sum/trace of gravitational coupling strengths over passive modes may be bounded by a mass-inertia functional,

```math
\mathcal R
\stackrel{?}{\lesssim}
\frac{G}{c^5} I\,\Omega^4,
```

with a definite numerical coefficient after normalization is fixed.

The previous conversational exploration suggested

```math
\mathcal R
\stackrel{?}{\le}
\frac{4G}{3c^5}I\Omega^4,
```

but this coefficient is **unverified**.

## H3 — compact TT propagation resource

The normalized separated compact quadrupolar TT channel may obey a leading wave-zone operator bound

```math
\|P_g(\omega)\|_{\rm op}^2
\stackrel{?}{\le}
\frac{25}{16[k(\omega)R]^2}.
```

This must be reconstructed independently rather than imported from Experiment 01, because the operational normalization may differ between state capture, power transfer, and the throughput metric used here.

## H4 — two-ended inertia closure

If H1–H3 are all correct in compatible normalizations, then a narrowband combination may yield

```math
\Gamma_{\rm coh}
\stackrel{?}{\lesssim}
\frac{25G\omega^2}{12c^3R^2}
\min(I_A,I_B).
```

This is the principal candidate theorem. It is not currently a repository claim.

## H5 — passive many-mode mixing does not create oscillator strength

Unitary/passive hybridization may redistribute coupling among normal modes without increasing the total gravitational coupling trace.

This should be tested against strongly hybridized multimode resonant-mass models rather than assumed from finite-dimensional intuition.

## H6 — repeated passive returns do not improve the retained leading wave-zone ceiling

Repeated scattering between the same two compact passive endpoints may alter higher-order terms while leaving the leading `1/R^2` upper-bound coefficient unchanged.

Only an inequality is sought initially. No equality for the actual recurrent transfer should be presumed.

## H7 — novelty is at most in the complete closure

Even if H4 survives, likely ingredients include established gravitational-antenna theory, modal participation/sum rules, passive systems theory, and generic source–receiver wave bounds.

The novelty question is therefore not whether the ingredients are new, but whether the complete gravity-specific two-ended inertia closure is already known.

A negative search is not proof of priority.
