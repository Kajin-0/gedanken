# 2. What they explicitly study

They consider

- two massive particles;
- each confined in a harmonic oscillator potential;
- separated by a fixed distance;
- both coupled to a quantized propagating gravitational field.

They derive reduced matter dynamics by tracing out the graviton field using an operator/Feynman–Vernon influence-functional treatment.

Their stated central result is that the propagating quantized gravitational field can generate entanglement between the two matter systems.

Crucially, they also report that the entanglement does **not** appear instantaneously: its onset is delayed by the source–receiver separation, reflecting causal propagation of the gravitational field.

Therefore the broad conceptual claim

> propagating gravitons can causally generate entanglement between spatially separated quantum matter systems after a distance-dependent delay

is already occupied.

# 3. Their interaction model

They quantize the TT gravitational field and use the standard linear interaction

$$
H_{\rm int}
=-\frac12\int d^3x\,
h_{ij}T_{ij}.
$$

For their two one-dimensional trapped particles they focus on the

$$
T_{11}
$$

sector associated with motion along the oscillator axis.

They explicitly state that this is an effective treatment emphasizing the **causal propagating contribution** rather than a complete treatment of the full tensorial structure of the graviton interaction.

Their reduced dynamics retains two-time field correlations rather than assuming an instantaneous gravitational interaction.

# 4. What this kills in Experiment 01

Do **not** claim as new:

1. that propagating gravitons can mediate matter–matter entanglement;
2. that such entanglement can have a causal distance-dependent onset;
3. that one can retain propagation time explicitly in a quantized-gravity entanglement calculation;
4. that harmonic matter degrees of freedom can become entangled through propagating quantum gravitational modes;
5. the general statement that causal retardation itself distinguishes a radiative quantum-gravity entanglement protocol from instantaneous Newtonian models.

These are now explicit prior art.
