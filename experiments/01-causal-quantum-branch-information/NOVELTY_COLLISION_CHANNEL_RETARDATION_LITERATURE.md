## 2. Mari–Zippilli–Vitali (2026): direct collision with the broad channel framing

**Andrea Mari, Stefano Zippilli, David Vitali, “Can gravity mediate the transmission of quantum information?”, Phys. Rev. D 113, L021905 (2026), arXiv:2504.05998.**

Their central framework is already:

$$
\boxed{
\text{gravity quantum/nonclassical}
\Longleftarrow
\text{gravity-induced channel is non-entanglement-breaking}.
}
$$

They consider two optomechanical systems coupled only by gravity and define a gravity-induced optical transmission channel through **gravitationally induced transparency (GIT)**.

Under a quadratic gravitational Hamiltonian / weak Newtonian interaction and the rotating-wave approximation, the induced optical channel is a phase-insensitive **thermal attenuator** with transmissivity $\eta(\omega)$ and effective thermal occupation $N(\omega)$.

Their sharp condition is

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\eta}{(1-\eta)N}>1,
}
$$

which is exactly

$$
\eta>(1-\eta)N.
$$

In the notation used in Experiment 01,

$$
\tau=\eta,
\qquad
m=(1-\eta)N,
$$

so their boundary is exactly

$$
\boxed{
\tau>m.
}
$$

They explicitly discuss the equivalence, for this thermal attenuator, between non-entanglement-breaking behavior and quantum-information transmission capability.

### Experimental protocols already present in Mari et al.

They propose:

1. coherent input signals to estimate transmissivity and thermal background noise;
2. a model-independent coherent-state fidelity benchmark;
3. sending one mode of an entangled two-mode squeezed state and detecting output–ancilla entanglement.

Therefore Experiment 01 must **not** claim that coherent probes, EB characterization, or ancilla-assisted entanglement distribution through a gravitational channel are new.

The searchable full text contains no explicit treatment of

## 8. Cliche–Kempf (2009): relativistic communication channel causality already exists

**Mathieu Cliche, Achim Kempf, “The relativistic quantum channel of communication through field quanta,” Phys. Rev. A 81, 012330 (2010), arXiv:0908.3144.**

They explicitly construct the CPTP channel from a localized sender detector to a localized receiver detector through a scalar quantum field.

Using microcausality, they show that when the interaction regions are spacelike separated the receiver state is independent of the sender input.

They also emphasize the essential distinction that

- the communication channel has zero classical/quantum capacity for spacelike separation;
- the two detectors can nevertheless become entangled through pre-existing vacuum correlations.

This is extremely close to the logic of our `MICROCAUSAL_REPLACER_THEOREM.md`.

Therefore the generic statement

$$
\boxed{
\text{source-controlled channel is input-independent outside the light cone}

Our use of the entanglement-breaking set / EB robustness as a front variable is a convenient formulation, but should not be presented as a foundational discovery of relativistic channel causality.
