# 1. Abstract — current recommended version

Quantum gravitational radiation, gravity-mediated quantum communication, propagating-graviton entanglement, and quantum gravitational-wave reception have each been studied in related but usually separate models. We construct a source-resolved benchmark that joins these elements in a single weak-field calculation. A degenerate local reference qubit and a branch-common work mode prepare opposite coherent amplitudes of a finite-mass four-spoke elastic quadrupole through an internal sign-controlled resonant interaction. The controller can return to the same vacuum state in both branches, while the distributed elastic realization keeps its leading local energy density branch common. The source stress tensor is conserved, and for \(q=\omega L/c_s\) the branch-difference quadrupole is

$$
\Delta Q_{xx}=8\mu Lu\frac{\tan q}{q},
\qquad
\Delta Q_{yy}=-\Delta Q_{xx},
$$

with generalized mode mass

$$
M_{\rm eff}=4\mu\left(\frac12+\frac{q}{\sin2q}\right).
$$

The corresponding gravitational linewidth is

$$
\kappa_g(q)
=\frac{8G\mu L^2\omega^4}{5c^5}
\frac{(\tan q/q)^2}{\frac12+q/\sin2q}.
$$

All gravitational radiation emitted during local encoding and subsequent passive decay can be compressed into one normalized outgoing temporal mode. For vacuum nongravitational source ports, the complete source-to-graviton stage is equivalent to a pure-loss channel with gravitational branching fraction

$$
\eta_g=\kappa_{g,A}/\kappa_A.
$$

In the aligned compact-source wave zone, the normalized source-mode storage fraction of a resonant quadrupole receiver is

$$
\eta_{\rm store}
=\frac{25\mathcal O}{16(kR)^2}
$$

at leading order. The receiver therefore realizes a time-dependent phase-insensitive Gaussian map whose coherent parameter is obtained by convolving the complete retarded source waveform with the receiver impulse response. For the locally prepared binary-coherent probe, established Gaussian-channel results imply that source-reference entanglement survives at the receiver exactly when

$$
\eta_g\tau_{\rm full}(t)>m_B(t)
$$

in the vacuum-source-loss model. For a freely decaying exponential source and a constant-coupling passive receiver, linewidth matching is globally optimal and gives the compact end-to-end ceiling

$$
\tau_{\rm passive}^{\max}
=4e^{-2}\beta_{g,A}\beta_{g,B}\eta_{\rm store}
=\frac{25e^{-2}}{4}
\frac{\mathcal O}{(kR)^2}
\beta_{g,A}\beta_{g,B}.
$$

We bound finite-support, nonlinear-quadrupole, controller, thermal-encoding, and reciprocal-feedback corrections and distinguish the exact causal support of the source operation from pre-existing field correlations. The resulting construction is a theoretical normalization benchmark rather than a near-term experimental proposal.

# 4. Conserved finite-support quadrupole source

Use four endpoint masses \(\mu\) connected to a central compact hub by four identical longitudinal elastic spokes of reference length \(L\).

For one spoke, normalize the longitudinal normal-mode shape to unit endpoint displacement:

$$
\boxed{
f_q(x)=\frac{\sin(qx/L)}{\sin q}.}
$$

The endpoint traction condition gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{m_r}{\mu}=q\tan q.}
$$

For plus coordinate \(u\), choose the mirrored pattern

$$
\xi_x=+u f_q(x),
\qquad
\xi_y=-u f_q(x),
$$

with the opposite branch obtained by \(u\to-u\).

The total branch-difference STF plus quadrupole, including spoke rest mass, is

$$
\boxed{
\Delta Q_{xx}
=8\mu Lu\frac{\tan q}{q},
\qquad
\Delta Q_{yy}=-\Delta Q_{xx},
\qquad
\Delta Q_{zz}=0.
}
$$

For \(q\ll1\),

$$
\frac{\tan q}{q}
=1+\frac{q^2}{3}+\frac{2q^4}{15}+O(q^6).
$$

Thus the finite support required by local momentum conservation does not cancel the endpoint quadrupole; in this explicit mode it reinforces it.

The source is described by

$$
T_{\rm tot}^{\mu\nu}
=T_{\rm end}^{\mu\nu}
+T_{\rm spokes}^{\mu\nu}
+T_{\rm hub}^{\mu\nu}
+T_{\rm ctrl}^{\mu\nu},
$$

with

$$
\boxed{\partial_\mu T_{\rm tot}^{\mu\nu}=0}
$$

to the working order of the elastic model.

For a compact conserved source,

$$
\ddot I_{ij}=2\int d^3x\,T_{ij},
$$

so internal stress and the total energy quadrupole are two representations of the same leading radiative source rather than independent contributions to be added afterward.

# 16. Causal statement

The fundamental causal object is the support of the **source operation**, not the assertion that all source-receiver correlations vanish outside a light cone.

Let \(\mathcal W_A\) be the source-operation worldtube and \(\mathcal O_B(t)\) the receiver observation region. If they are spacelike separated, microcausality implies that the receiver output is independent of the controllable source input. The source-controlled map is therefore a replacer channel in that region.

For a centrally triggered finite source, if source point \(\mathbf x\) cannot respond before

$$
t_x\ge\frac{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|}{c},
$$

then a contribution reaching receiver point \(\mathbf y\) satisfies

$$
t_{\rm arr}
\ge
\frac{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0|+|\mathbf y-\mathbf x|}{c}
\ge
\frac{|\mathbf y-\mathbf x_0|}{c}.
$$

Thus a center-origin \(R/c\) lower bound remains valid for a causally triggered extended source.

For a generic distributed intervention, state the causal front using the minimum separation of the operation and receiver supports rather than center-to-center distance.

Pre-existing vacuum correlations or static dressing do not contradict this statement because the claim concerns **receiver dependence on the later local source operation**.

# 17. Reciprocal gravitational backaction

The source and receiver couple reciprocally through the same retarded Green function. The one-way cascade is the first Born term, not a fundamental directional assumption.

For frequency offset \(\nu\), define

$$
\chi_j(\nu)=\frac1{\kappa_j/2-i\nu}.
$$

The round-trip loop gain is

$$
\boxed{
L(\nu)
=\Sigma_{AB}^R\Sigma_{BA}^R
\chi_A(\nu)\chi_B(\nu)
 e^{2i\nu R/c}.}
$$

The exact reciprocal response is the one-way response multiplied by

$$
\frac1{1+L(\nu)}
$$

up to sign convention.

Using

$$
|\Sigma_{AB}^R|^2
=\kappa_{g,A}\kappa_{g,B}\eta_{\rm store},
$$

one obtains

$$
\boxed{
|L(\nu)|
\le
4\eta_{\rm store}\beta_{g,A}\beta_{g,B}.}
$$

Therefore, in the wave zone, the first reciprocal correction is \(O((kR)^{-2})\) relative to the forward transfer amplitude and \(O((kR)^{-4})\) in absolute transfer probability.

Causally, the first source-controlled feedback echo cannot return to the receiver before the path

$$
A\to B\to A\to B
$$

has elapsed, i.e. approximately \(3R/c\) for compact separated devices. The initial direct reception window is therefore exactly one-way with respect to source-controlled round-trip feedback.

# 21. Physical interpretation

Three different quantities must never be conflated.

## Source branch strength

$$
N_\Delta.
$$

This controls the distinguishability of the two emitted graviton histories and the magnitude of source-reference entanglement available for transfer.

## Channel quality

$$
\tau_{A\to B}-m_{A\to B}.
$$

This determines whether the channel can preserve entanglement at all.

## Practical certification

A real measurement requires a nonzero witness margin above experimental/statistical uncertainty. A mathematically non-EB channel with an astronomically small coherent amplitude need not be practically certifiable.

The passive laboratory benchmarks in this repository remain extraordinarily weak. The paper is therefore a structural theoretical result, not a claim of near-term observability.
