# 2. Closest inspected works

## 2.1 Laga and Suyama (2026)

Felix Laga and Teruaki Suyama,
**“Quantum description of gravitational waves generated by a classical source,”**
arXiv:2604.20228.

They quantize the gravitational-wave field driven by a classical energy-momentum tensor and show that the field expectation value reproduces the classical retarded solution. They also analyze graviton-number statistics and obtain coherent/Poisson behavior.

**Stage covered:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{specified stress-energy source}
\rightarrow
\text{quantized gravitational radiation}.
}
$$

**Not supplied:** a remote matter receiver, receiver branching, readout, or the complete serial source-to-receiver normalization.

---

## 2.2 Matsui (2026)

Hiroki Matsui,
**“Graviton-induced which-path decoherence in matter-wave interferometry,”**
arXiv:2607.20867.

This is especially close on the source side. The paper solves branch-dependent graviton displacements for a closed conserved source and shows that in vacuum the decoherence exponent is half the mean graviton number radiated by the difference source.

It also states that its quadrupole formula strictly applies to the complete conserved matter--apparatus source, while its numerical estimates neglect setup-dependent branch contributions from the apparatus.

**Stage covered:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{branch source}
\rightarrow
\text{radiative graviton difference state}
\rightarrow
\text{source decoherence}.
}
$$

**Not supplied:** a separately normalized remote capture interface and accessible receiver channel.

The V7 finite-spoke/controller architecture addresses precisely the apparatus-normalization issue that Matsui leaves setup dependent in the estimate stage.

## 2.3 Toccacelo, Beitel, Andersen, and Pikovski (2026)

Kristian Toccacelo, Thomas Beitel, Ulrik Lund Andersen, and Igor Pikovski,
**“Quantum State Characterization of Gravitational Waves via Graviton Counting Statistics,”**
arXiv:2602.09125.

They treat a quantized gravitational wave incident on a macroscopic bulk acoustic resonator. In the RWA the graviton--phonon interaction is a beam-splitter Hamiltonian and they explicitly study state transfer from the gravitational mode into the detector, including detector states and statistics.

**Stage covered:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{already incident normalized GW mode}
\rightarrow
\text{resonant matter receiver}.
}
$$

**Not supplied:** the distant physical source that produced the incident mode, its gravitational branching fraction, or the geometrical source-to-receiver normalization connecting the two interfaces.

This distinction is exactly why the receiver-local $\sim10^{-22}$ scale and V7's ordinary-source/ordinary-receiver $\sim10^{-42}$ scale are not the same quantity.

## 2.4 Tobar et al. (2024)

Germain Tobar, Sreenath K. Manikandan, Thomas Beitel, and Igor Pikovski,
**“Detecting single gravitons with quantum sensing,”**
*Nature Communications* **15**, 7229 (2024), arXiv:2308.15440.

This establishes stimulated and spontaneous single-graviton processes in massive acoustic resonators and motivates quantum-jump detection of passing gravitational waves.

**Stage covered:** gravitational wave / graviton $\leftrightarrow$ local acoustic detector.

**Not supplied:** a complete separated local emitter-to-detector quantum link with both matter branching efficiencies.

## 2.5 Trenggana and Zen (2026)

Anom Trenggana and Freddy P. Zen,
**“Quantum Gravity Induced Entanglement from Propagating Gravitons,”**
arXiv:2606.12901.

They study two separated harmonic matter systems coupled through propagating modes of the quantized gravitational field and find a distance-dependent delay before entanglement appears.

Importantly, they explicitly describe the interaction used in the paper as an **effective form focused on causal propagation**, with greater emphasis on field propagation than on a complete tensorial treatment of the graviton--matter source interaction.

**Stage covered:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{two separated matter systems}
\leftrightarrow
\text{propagating quantum gravitational field}
}
$$

in an influence-functional reduced-dynamics treatment.

**Not supplied:** V7's locally prepared conserved radiative source, source gravitational branching, normalized selected emitted mode, serial storage factor, and separate receiver/readout bookkeeping.

Therefore this paper kills any broad claim that V7 is the first causal propagating-graviton matter-entanglement construction, but it does not duplicate the V7 normalization problem.

## 2.6 Mari, Zippilli, and Vitali (2026)

Andrea Mari, Stefano Zippilli, and David Vitali,
**“Can gravity mediate the transmission of quantum information?”**
*Physical Review D* **113**, L021905 (2026), arXiv:2504.05998.

They construct a full optical input-to-output channel through two gravitationally coupled optomechanical systems, and analyze its entanglement-breaking threshold.

Their explicit model uses a direct quadratic gravitational coupling

$$
V=\hbar\lambda
(b_1+b_1^\dagger)(b_2+b_2^\dagger),
$$

with the Newtonian interaction as the central example. In the RWA this becomes a chain of coherently hopping optical/mechanical modes.

**Stage covered:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{optical input}
\rightarrow
\text{mechanical system 1}
\rightarrow
\text{effective gravitational coupling}
\rightarrow
\text{mechanical system 2}
\rightarrow
\text{optical output}.
}
$$

This is extremely important prior art for gravitational quantum-channel capability.

**Different normalization problem:** it does not resolve the interaction into

$$
\text{source graviton branching}
\times
\text{travelling-wave free-space mode capture}
\times
\text{receiver graviton branching}.
$$

Thus V7 should cite this paper prominently but should not describe its channel/EB framework itself as novel.

# 4. Current novelty boundary

The following claims should **not** be made:

- gravity can transmit quantum information;
- non-EB gravitational channels are new;
- propagating gravitons can causally entangle distant matter is new;
- coherent graviton radiation from a source is new;
- quantum graviton transduction is new;
- graviton absorption/state transfer into a mechanical receiver is new;
- the critical quadrupole absorption area is new.

The defensible V7 contribution remains:

> **A source-resolved weak-linearized-gravity construction that closes local preparation, conserved radiative emission, normalized travelling-mode propagation, remote memory capture, Gaussian noise, and accessible readout in one serial normalization, exposing the separate source and receiver gravitational branching penalties.**

The most quantitatively important consequence is that a receiver-local incoming-mode estimate can differ from a true ordinary-matter end-to-end estimate by the entire source gravitational branching factor.
