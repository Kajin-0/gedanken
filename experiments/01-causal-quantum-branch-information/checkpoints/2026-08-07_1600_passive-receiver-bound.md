## Corrected receiver theorem

For any stationary **passive** nonrelativistic receiver,

$$
\rho=\sum_mp_m|m\rangle\langle m|,
\qquad
E_m<E_n\Rightarrow p_m\ge p_n,
$$

the STF quadrupole spectrum obeys

$$
\boxed{
\sum_A\sum_{m<n}
(p_m-p_n)(E_n-E_m)|Q^A_{mn}|^2
=\frac{10}{3}\hbar^2\langle I\rangle_\rho.
}
$$

Thus the positive-frequency quadrupole response has a finite energy-weighted budget, and for a narrow band

$$
\boxed{
\kappa_{g,\rm net}
\lesssim
\frac{4G}{3c^5}\langle I\rangle_\rho\omega_B^4.
}
$$

This extends the earlier ground-state receiver ceiling to thermal/passive stationary receivers.

## Active loophole

The bound is not universal for arbitrary prepared many-body states. Existing collective-atom work finds selected states with gravitational transition rates scaling as $N^2$.

For a nonpassive state define positive and inverted spectral budgets $S_+$ and $S_-$. The exact identity is

$$
\boxed{
S_+-S_-
=\frac{10}{3}\hbar^2\langle I\rangle_\rho.
}
$$

Therefore response above the passive ceiling requires an active/inverted spectral resource.
