## Scalar model with finite receiver memory

Including probe amplitude damping $\kappa_B$, the narrow-band retarded response mode decays backward from the final interrogation time. If the complementary record spectrum is approximately constant over the probe linewidth,

$$
\eta_T
\simeq
\mathcal C_{\rm hist}
\left(1-e^{-\kappa_B(T-R/c)}\right),
$$

with

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal C_{\rm hist}
=\frac{|\mathcal R_B(\omega_B,R)|^2}
{\kappa_BS_E(\omega_B)}.
}
$$

A positive optimized strong history-transfer witness is possible only if

$$
\boxed{\mathcal C_{\rm hist}>1.}
$$

When this holds,

$$
\boxed{
T_*(R)
\simeq
\frac{R}{c}
-
\frac{1}{\kappa_B}
\ln\left(1-\frac{1}{\mathcal C_{\rm hist}}\right).
}
$$

For $\mathcal C_{\rm hist}\gg1$ the buildup term reduces to $1/\gamma_{\rm hist}$.

### Interpretation

The receiver has finite quantum memory. A source can only create a strong positive history-transfer margin if coherent branch information enters the probe faster than the combined complementary-record leakage and receiver memory loss.

### Gaussian source result

For a Gaussian scalar source profile, the same spatial form factor appears in the far-field retarded response and in the source radiation spectrum. In the narrow-band ratio $|\mathcal R_B|^2/S_E$, source strength and source-size form factors cancel. The ideal threshold is therefore chiefly a property of receiver coupling, receiver frequency/memory, separation, and field channel.

### Temperature

Equilibrium field noise enhances the record spectrum by the usual fluctuation-dissipation factor. Schematically,

$$
S_E^{(T)}(\omega)
=S_E^{(0)}(\omega)
\coth\left(\frac{\hbar\omega}{2k_BT}\right),
$$

so the history-transfer efficiency is reduced by the corresponding $\tanh$ factor.

### Pure-loss interpretation

For a passive effective channel with transmissivity $\tau_{\rm ch}$,

$$
\mathcal M_\Xi=(2\tau_{\rm ch}-1)|\Delta|^2,
$$

so the strong witness requires $\tau_{\rm ch}>1/2$, matching the pure-loss degradable/antidegradable boundary. This is a strong sufficient witness, not the general threshold for existence of entanglement.
