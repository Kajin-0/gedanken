## New central variable: history-transfer margin

For the balanced source-probe state

$$
\rho_{AB}=\frac12
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho_L & \Xi\\
\Xi^\dagger & \rho_R
\end{pmatrix},
$$

define

$$
C_\Xi=\|\Xi\|_1,
\qquad
D_B=\frac12\|\rho_L-\rho_R\|_1.
$$

The separable-state bound

$$
C_\Xi^2+D_B^2\le1
$$

is equivalently

$$
\boxed{
\chi_B\le2\Gamma_\Xi,
}
$$

with

$$
\chi_B=-\ln(1-D_B^2),
\qquad
\Gamma_\Xi=-\ln C_\Xi.
$$

Define

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal M_\Xi
=\chi_B-2\Gamma_\Xi
=\ln\left(\frac{C_\Xi^2}{1-D_B^2}\right).
}
$$

Then $\mathcal M_\Xi>0$ certifies entanglement. This logarithmic form is preferred because field-theory response and decoherence enter as additive exponents.

## Complementary-output interpretation

For pure conditional global histories $|\Phi_L\rangle_{BE}$ and $|\Phi_R\rangle_{BE}$,

$$
\Xi=\operatorname{Tr}_E|\Phi_L\rangle\langle\Phi_R|.
$$

A standard Uhlmann/trace-norm identity gives

$$
\boxed{\|\Xi\|_1=F(\rho_E^L,\rho_E^R).}
$$

Thus $C_\Xi$ quantifies how indistinguishable the unobserved complementary records remain. This avoids identifying a fundamental separate gravitational-field subsystem.

## Scalar-field drive limit

For source branch-current difference $\Delta J_A$ and a probe oscillator coupled to a massless scalar field,

$$
\Delta F_B(t)
=\lambda_B\int d^4x'\,G_R(x_B,t;x')\Delta J_A(x'),
$$

and

$$
\Delta\alpha_B(T)
=\frac{i x_{\rm zpf}}{\hbar}
\int_0^Tdt\,e^{i\omega_Bt}\Delta F_B(t).
$$

In the coherent-drive / weak-backreaction limit,

$$
D_B^2=1-e^{-|\Delta\alpha_B|^2}.
$$

If the unobserved field histories differ by coherent displacement $\Delta\beta$,

$$
C_\Xi^2=e^{-\|\Delta\beta\|^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal M_\Xi
=|\Delta\alpha_B|^2-\|\Delta\beta\|^2.
}
$$

Interpretation: positive witness means more branch distinguishability has been coherently deposited in the probe than leaked into the unobserved complementary output.
