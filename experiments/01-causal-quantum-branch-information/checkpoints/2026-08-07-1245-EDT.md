### Separable-state history-coherence bound

For

$$
\rho_{AB}=\frac12
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho_L & \Xi\\
\Xi^\dagger & \rho_R
\end{pmatrix},
$$

define

$$
C_\Xi=\|\Xi\|_1,
\qquad
D_B=\frac12\|\rho_L-\rho_R\|_1.
$$

Every balanced separable state obeys

$$
\boxed{C_\Xi^2+D_B^2\le1.}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal W_\Xi=C_\Xi^2+D_B^2-1>0}
$$

certifies source-probe entanglement.

### Equivalent finite-time coherence cost

With

$$
\Gamma_\Xi=-\ln C_\Xi,
$$

separability implies

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_\Xi\ge-\frac12\ln(1-D_B^2)}.
$$

For $D_B\ll1$,

$$
\Gamma_\Xi\gtrsim D_B^2/2.
$$

This is the cleanest finite-time expression so far of the statement that a classicalized interaction must spend recoverable history coherence to provide branch information.

### Operational measurement

Trace-norm duality permits $C_\Xi$ to be estimated via optimized joint eraser correlations rather than full state tomography. A simpler sufficient witness uses measured lower bounds $c_U\le C_\Xi$ and $d_M\le D_B$:

$$
\boxed{c_U^2+d_M^2>1\Rightarrow\rho_{AB}\text{ entangled}.}
$$

Equivalently, for any angle $\vartheta$, separable states obey

$$
\boxed{c_U\cos\vartheta+d_M\sin\vartheta\le1.}
$$

This gives a family of ordinary linear entanglement witnesses.

### Causal version

For source-probe separation $R$, the source-controlled branch distinguishability obeys

$$
D_B(T,R)=0\quad(T<R/c).
$$

Define

$$
T_*(R)=\inf\{T:\mathcal W_\Xi(T,R)>0\}.
$$

A local theory requires

$$
T_*(R)\ge R/c.
$$

### Gravitational dressing caveat

A literal $A\otimes g\otimes B$ decomposition is not safe as the fundamental theorem statement in gravity. Gauge constraints and gravitational dressing make subsystem independence nontrivial. The final witness should therefore use only operational $A$-$B$ quantities $(\rho_L,\rho_R,\Xi)$; a separate mediator/environment overlap is only an interpretation when a valid Stinespring or gauge-fixed factorization exists.
