## New result

The branch-dependent coherent gravitational radiation generated by the two source histories can be reduced exactly to a single normalized bosonic **difference mode** $b_\Delta$ carrying all $L/R$ distinguishability.

Define

$$
N_\Delta=\sum_\lambda\int d^3k\,|\beta^L_{\mathbf k\lambda}-\beta^R_{\mathbf k\lambda}|^2.
$$

An enclosing receiver that coherently captures fraction $\eta$ of that mode produces

$$
D_B^2=1-e^{-\eta N_\Delta},
$$

$$
C_\Xi^2=e^{-(1-\eta)N_\Delta}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal W_\Xi
=e^{-(1-\eta)N_\Delta}-e^{-\eta N_\Delta}
}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal M_\Xi=(2\eta-1)N_\Delta.
}
$$

The strong history witness is positive iff

$$
\boxed{\eta>1/2.}
$$

This is **not** the entanglement threshold. Exact source-receiver negativity is positive for every finite $N_\Delta>0$ and every coherent capture $\eta>0$.

## Exact negativity

With

$$
s_E=e^{-(1-\eta)N_\Delta/2},
\qquad
s_B=e^{-\eta N_\Delta/2},

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal N_{AB}

\left[
\sqrt{(1+s_E)^2-4s_Es_B^2}-(1-s_E)

$$

Thus any nonzero coherent capture transfers some entanglement from the radiation field to the receiver.

Source-only visibility is

$$
\eta(T)=\eta_\infty\int_{-\infty}^{T-R/c}|f(t)|^2dt.
