## Experiment 01: Causal Transport of Quantum Branch Information by Gravity

### New result

In the weak one-way scalar-field coherent-drive model, write the source branch-current difference as

$$
\Delta J_A(\mathbf x,t)=s(\mathbf x)u(t).
$$

The finite-time retarded response mode seen by a harmonic probe is

$$
r_T(t')
=\frac{i\lambda_Bx_{\rm zpf}}{\hbar}
\int_{t'}^Tdt\,e^{i\omega_Bt}
\int d^3x'\,G_R(x_B,t;\mathbf x',t')s(\mathbf x').
$$

Then

$$
\Delta\alpha_B=\langle r_T,u\rangle,
\qquad
\chi_B=|\Delta\alpha_B|^2
=\langle u,K_Bu\rangle,
$$

with rank-one response kernel

$$
K_B=|r_T\rangle\langle r_T|.
$$

Write the complementary history-record cost as

$$
2\Gamma_\Xi=\langle u,N_Tu\rangle,
$$

where $N_T\succeq0$ is obtained from the Hadamard/noise sector. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal M_\Xi[u]
=\langle u,(K_B-N_T)u\rangle.
}
$$

This is the first explicit quadratic response-minus-record functional for the project.

### Optimization

Under a quadratic source-control budget

$$
\langle u,Wu\rangle=1,
$$

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal M_{\Xi,\max}
=\lambda_{\max}
\left[
W^{-1/2}(K_B-N_T)W^{-1/2}
\right].
}
$$

Because $K_B$ is rank one, if $N_T$ is invertible on the relevant support,

$$
\boxed{
\eta_T=\langle r_T,N_T^{-1}r_T\rangle>1
}
$$

is equivalent to the existence of a source history with positive margin and therefore source-probe entanglement.

The optimal complex-envelope history is

$$
\boxed{u_{\rm opt}\propto N_T^{-1}r_T.}
$$

Interpretation: the optimal trajectory is a **noise-whitened matched history**—the time-reversed retarded probe mode after whitening by the environment's ability to retain branch information.

### Causal scalar limit

For the ideal $3+1$D massless scalar propagator,

$$
G_R(R,t-t')\propto\frac{\delta(t-t'-R/c)}{4\pi R},
$$

so

$$
\chi_B(T,R)
\propto
\frac{\lambda_B^2x_{\rm zpf}^2}{16\pi^2\hbar^2R^2}
\left|
\int_0^{T-R/c}dt\,u(t)e^{i\omega_Bt}
\right|^2.
$$

Thus branch transfer is exactly zero before $R/c$ and afterward depends on the resonant projection of the source history onto the probe mode.

### Physical insight

The fluctuation-dissipation relation ties the field's equilibrium Hadamard/noise kernel to the dissipative/spectral part of the retarded response, while coherent transfer uses the full retarded kernel. The **reactive/dispersive near field** can therefore mediate coherent branch response with much less radiative record formation than an on-shell propagating signal would require.

This is likely the main physical lever of the experiment.

### Gravity specialization

Near-zone weak gravity gives

$$
\Delta F_B(t)
\simeq
\frac{2Gm_Am_B}{R^3}\Delta x_A(t-R/c),
$$

and therefore

$$
\Delta\alpha_B(T)
\simeq
\frac{2iGm_Am_Bx_{\rm zpf}}{\hbar R^3}
\int_0^{T-R/c}dt\,\Delta x_A(t)e^{i\omega_B(t+R/c)}.
$$

The matched-history result suggests resonant source modulation as a possible amplifier, but a gravity-specific radiation calculation must include the complete actuator/apparatus stress-energy. Resonant and parametric enhancement are already represented in the literature, so no novelty claim is attached to resonance itself.

### Novelty status

The generic mathematics—separability witness, influence functional, fluctuation-dissipation relation, and matched-filter optimization—is not presumed novel. The potential paper contribution is the gravity-specific synthesis:

$$

\text{retarded branch transfer}

}
$$
