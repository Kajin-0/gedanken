## Gravity-specific history-transfer margin

The logarithmic witness

$$
\mathcal M_\Xi=\chi_B-2\Gamma_\Xi
$$

has a direct interpretation in a coherent-radiation approximation.

For a probe oscillator with conditional states $|\pm\alpha\rangle$,

$$
\chi_B=4|\alpha|^2.
$$

For vacuum gravitational radiation, recent linearized-gravity calculations give a matter-wave decoherence exponent equal to half the mean number of gravitons radiated by the **difference source**,

$$
\Gamma_{\rm vac}=N_g/2.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal M_\Xi\simeq4|\alpha|^2-N_g.
}
$$

Interpretation: the strong witness becomes positive when the coherent branch record deposited in the intended probe exceeds the branch record leaked into unobserved radiative graviton modes.

## Half-period oscillator estimate

For the tidal source-probe coupling

$$
H_{\rm int}=\hbar g\sigma_z(a+a^\dagger),
$$

with

$$
g=\frac{Gm_Am_Bd\,x_{\rm zpf}}{\hbar R^3},
\qquad
x_{\rm zpf}=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m_B\omega}},
$$

the conditional displacement is maximal at half a mechanical period and

$$
4|\alpha|^2_{\rm max}=\frac{16g^2}{\omega^2}
=
\frac{8G^2m_A^2m_Bd^2}{\hbar R^6\omega^3}.
$$

For a smooth Gaussian source-splitting trajectory, Matsui (arXiv:2607.20867, 2026) gives

$$
\Gamma_{\rm vac}=\frac{8}{15}
\frac{Gm_A^2d^4}{\hbar c^5\tau_s^4},
$$

so

$$
N_g=\frac{16}{15}
\frac{Gm_A^2d^4}{\hbar c^5\tau_s^4}.
$$

The radiation-limited positive-margin condition becomes

$$
\boxed{
\tau_s^4>
\frac{2d^2R^6\omega^3}{15Gm_Bc^5}.
}
$$

Notably, the **sign threshold is independent of source mass $m_A$** because both coherent signal and radiative leakage scale as $m_A^2$. The magnitude of the margin still scales as $m_A^2$.

## Physical conclusion

For realistic smooth preparation times, the graviton-radiation term is fantastically small. Matsui reports $\Gamma_{\rm vac}\sim10^{-89}$ to $10^{-61}$ across representative existing matter-wave platforms. Thus radiative gravitons are a theoretically clean fundamental leakage channel but are almost certainly not the practical limitation; ordinary environmental decoherence will dominate.
