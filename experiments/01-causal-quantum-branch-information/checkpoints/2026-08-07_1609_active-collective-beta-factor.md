## Active collective correction

$$
\Gamma_{\rm gw}=\frac{N^2}{4}\Gamma_0N_{\rm gw},
$$

$$
\Gamma_{\rm vac}=\frac{N^2}{4}\Gamma_0.
$$

Thus the $N^2$ enhancement accelerates both stochastic gravitational-wave response and vacuum gravitational transitions. It does not improve their ratio.
