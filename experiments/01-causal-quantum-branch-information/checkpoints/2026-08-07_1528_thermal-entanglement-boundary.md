## Core new result

For the weak wave-zone source-cat state

$$
|\Psi\rangle
=\frac{|L\rangle|+a\rangle+|R\rangle|-a\rangle}{\sqrt2},
$$

sent through a thermal attenuator with transmissivity $\eta$ and environment occupation $\bar n$, direct partial-transpose analysis gives source-receiver entanglement iff

$$
\boxed{
\eta>\frac{\bar n}{\bar n+1}
}
$$

in the infinitesimal branch-separation limit.

This is exactly the established entanglement-breaking boundary of the bosonic thermal attenuator.

The simpler fidelity-history witness requires

$$
\boxed{
\eta>\frac{2\bar n+1}{2\bar n+2}.
}
$$

Hence there are three thermal regions:

1. $\eta\le\bar n/(\bar n+1)$: entanglement impossible through the thermal channel;
2. $\bar n/(\bar n+1)<\eta\le(2\bar n+1)/(2\bar n+2)$: entanglement exists for a weak source cat but the simple history witness fails;
3. $\eta>(2\bar n+1)/(2\bar n+2)$: the low-cost fidelity-history witness certifies it.

For a matched receiver memory,

$$
\eta=\frac{\kappa_g}{\kappa_g+\kappa_i},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{
\text{entanglement possible: }\kappa_g>\bar n_i\kappa_i
}
$$

whereas

$$
\boxed{
\text{simple fidelity witness: }\kappa_g>(2\bar n_i+1)\kappa_i.
}
$$

## Novelty boundary

The thermal attenuator entanglement-breaking threshold is established channel theory and is used explicitly in Mari, Zippilli, and Vitali (PRD 113, L021905, 2026). Do not claim that threshold as novel.

Potentially distinctive is the causal gravitational-wave history-transfer framing and the explicit separation between causal arrival, fundamental entanglement transfer, and low-cost operational certification.
