# Cover Letter — Physical Review D

**Manuscript:** *A Source-Resolved Quantum Link Budget for Propagating Linearized Gravity*

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, **“A Source-Resolved Quantum Link Budget for Propagating Linearized Gravity,”** for publication in *Physical Review D*.

The manuscript addresses a normalization problem that arises when gravitational quantum communication is treated end to end rather than beginning with an already prepared incoming gravitational mode. Existing literature has separately developed coherent graviton radiation from matter sources, propagating-graviton entanglement, quantum gravitational-wave reception and transduction, and entanglement-breaking benchmarks for gravitational channels. Our aim is to close the interfaces between these descriptions in one explicit weak-field construction.

We begin from a locally initialized two-dimensional source code whose total Poincaré generators act identically on the logical subspace to the retained perturbative order. A finite-support elastic quadrupole and a local finite-speed controller field provide an explicit conserved source realization. After the controller is coherently cleared, the complete branch-dependent coherent field can be compressed into one virtual bosonic difference mode. Its transfer to a distant resonant receiver factorizes as

$$
\tau_c(t)
=
\beta_{g,A}
\eta_{\rm store}(R)
\beta_{g,B}
\mathcal T_f(t),
$$

where the factors represent source gravitational branching, normalized free-space mode capture, receiver gravitational branching, and temporal loading. A separate noisy readout stage then determines whether the captured quantum excess remains accessible.

This source-resolved normalization changes the interpretation of receiver-local estimates by an entire source gravitational branching factor. For a deliberately aggressive ordinary-matter benchmark, a receiver-local coherent scale of order $10^{-22}$ becomes an end-to-end scale of order $10^{-42}$ when the source interface is retained. In vacuum we also derive the exact reference–receiver negativity for the resulting binary-coherent pure-loss channel and find the weak-link optimum

$$
\mathcal N_{\max}
=\eta-2\eta^{3/2}+O(\eta^2),
$$

so the deliverable entanglement remains of the same asymptotic order as the complete transmissivity.

The manuscript treats gravitational dressing within a deliberately restricted perturbative scope. The causal source code is formulated in an equal-Poincaré-charge subspace, and only a common first-order asymptotic dressing is assumed. We also bound finite source support, finite-speed controller propagation, finite hub/controller residuals, reciprocal feedback, passive-matter gravitational oscillator strength, source dephasing, and noisy readout. The free-space coefficient is independently checked through retarded-field, reciprocal-absorption, and canonical TT one-graviton normalizations.

We have kept the novelty claim narrow. We do **not** claim a new Gaussian-channel entanglement-breaking theorem, a new graviton transducer mechanism, or a new universal gravitational absorption cross section. The contribution is the source-resolved gravitational quantum-link construction and the quantitative consequences of retaining both matter–gravity interfaces in one normalization.

We believe this work is suited to *Physical Review D* because its main content concerns linearized gravity, gravitational radiation, quantum matter–gravity interfaces, causal propagation, and the operational structure of quantum gravitational channels.
