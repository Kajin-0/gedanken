# Draft Cover Letter — Physical Review D

**Manuscript:** *A Source-Resolved Quantum Link Budget for Propagating Linearized Gravity*

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, **“A Source-Resolved Quantum Link Budget for Propagating Linearized Gravity,”** for publication in *Physical Review D*.

The manuscript addresses a normalization problem that appears when gravitational quantum communication is treated end to end rather than beginning with an already prepared incoming gravitational mode. Existing literature has separately developed coherent graviton radiation from matter sources, propagating-graviton entanglement, quantum gravitational-wave reception and transduction, and entanglement-breaking benchmarks for gravitational channels. Our aim is not to replace those results, but to close the interfaces between them in one explicit weak-field construction.

We begin from a locally initialized two-dimensional source code whose total Poincare generators act identically on the logical subspace to the retained perturbative order. A finite-support elastic quadrupole and a local finite-speed controller field provide an explicit conserved source realization. After the controller is coherently cleared, the complete branch-dependent coherent field can be compressed into one virtual bosonic difference mode. Its transfer to a distant resonant receiver then factorizes as

$$
\tau_c(t)
=
\beta_{g,A}
\eta_{\rm store}(R)
\beta_{g,B}
\mathcal T_f(t),
$$

where the factors represent source gravitational branching, normalized free-space mode capture, receiver gravitational branching, and temporal loading. The receiver-memory channel is then followed by a separate noisy readout stage, so capture is not conflated with accessible quantum information.

This source-resolved normalization changes the interpretation of receiver-local estimates. For a deliberately aggressive ordinary-matter benchmark, a receiver-local coherent scale of order

$$
10^{-22}
$$

becomes an end-to-end scale of order

$$
10^{-42}
$$

when the source gravitational branching penalty is included as well. In vacuum we also derive the exact reference-receiver negativity for the resulting binary-coherent pure-loss channel and show that its optimized weak-link value scales linearly with the complete transmissivity,

$$
\mathcal N_{\max}
=\eta-2\eta^{3/2}+O(\eta^2).
$$

The paper also treats gravitational dressing carefully: because gravity does not admit the same compact subsystem structure as an ordinary local field theory, the causal source code is formulated in an equal-Poincare-charge subspace and only a first-order common asymptotic dressing is assumed. We additionally bound finite source support, finite-speed controller propagation, reciprocal feedback, passive-matter gravitational oscillator strength, source dephasing, and noisy readout.

We have tried to keep the novelty claim narrow. We do **not** claim a new Gaussian-channel entanglement-breaking theorem, a new graviton transducer mechanism, or a new universal gravitational absorption cross section. The contribution is the source-resolved gravitational quantum-link construction and the quantitative consequences of retaining both matter-gravity interfaces in one normalization.

We believe this work is suited to *Physical Review D* because the main content concerns linearized gravity, gravitational radiation, quantum matter-gravity interfaces, causal propagation, and the operational structure of quantum gravitational channels.
