## Terminology clarification

The earlier phrase **“external review”** referred to repository-level adversarial review passes used to harden the manuscript. These included independent AI-agent critiques, direct equation checks, primary-source comparisons, numerical regressions, and prior-art searches.

They were **not formal journal peer review** and are not presented as endorsement by an external physicist, journal, or institution.
