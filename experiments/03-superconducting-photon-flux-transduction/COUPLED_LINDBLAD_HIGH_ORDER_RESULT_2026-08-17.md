## Interpretation

Established:

1. the p16 corrected bath is generated by a physical coupled-Lindblad auxiliary
   system under the published SDP conditions;
2. its time-domain correlation agrees with the exact direct-port bath at the
   ~5.1e-4 relative level on the frozen 0<=tau<=24 grid;
3. p8->p12->p16 convergence is coherent in correction size, exact C(t),
   system-band detailed balance, and normalized spectrum metrics;
4. the construction is computationally small at bath level (18 auxiliary
   modes for p16).

Not established:

- exact harmonic system equilibrium under this approximate bath;
- nonlinear detector dynamics;
- nonlinear auxiliary-mode/Fock cutoff convergence;
- pulse capture probability;
- exact quantum efficiency;
- publication-level novelty.

## Next stage

The coupled-Lindblad bath is authorized for a **harmonic system benchmark only**.

Because the harmonic system plus all coupled auxiliary bosonic modes is a linear
Gaussian open system, the first benchmark should solve its covariance/Lyapunov
dynamics directly.  That calculation is exact in the auxiliary bosonic Hilbert
space and therefore introduces no finite Fock cutoff.

For the later nonlinear detector, explicit auxiliary Fock/local-basis cutoff
convergence remains mandatory; the Gaussian harmonic result does not waive that
requirement.

The harmonic benchmark must compare p12 and p16 against the exact direct-port
Gaussian/FDT state and retain the existing Gate-B full-state standards where
