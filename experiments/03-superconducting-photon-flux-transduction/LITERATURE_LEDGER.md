# Experiment 03 — LITERATURE_LEDGER

**Purpose:** primary literature boundary for architecture and novelty work. This is a seed, not a completed collision audit.

## A. Single-photon superconducting detectors in the MIR/LWIR/FIR

### Verma et al., APL Photonics 6, 056101 (2021)

**Title:** Single-photon detection in the mid-infrared up to 10 µm wavelength using tungsten silicide superconducting nanowire detectors  
**DOI:** 10.1063/5.0048049

Relevant result: WSi SNSPDs showed saturated internal detection efficiency out to 10 µm. This establishes that single 10-µm photons can be detected with superconducting matter; Experiment 03 must not claim LWIR superconducting single-photon detection itself as new.

### Day et al., Phys. Rev. X 14, 041005 (2024)

**Title:** A 25-micrometer Single-Photon-Sensitive Kinetic Inductance Detector  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevX.14.041005

Relevant result: individual 25-µm photons were resolved in pulse-counting mode; integrating operation remained photon-noise limited over a very large absorbed-power range. This is a strong benchmark against claims that superconductivity uniquely enables the Experiment-03 sensitivity regime.

## B. Photon-triggered Josephson calorimetry

### Lee et al., Nature Communications 17, 3845 (2026)

**Title:** Thermal detection of single photons using Dirac fermions  
**Article:** s41467-026-70648-0

Relevant result: a graphene-based hybrid Josephson junction detects absorbed 1550-nm photons through the very low heat capacity of Dirac electrons and thermally triggered Josephson switching. Reported intrinsic efficiency reaches 87%, with lower-efficiency operating points permitting very low dark counts. The graphene area is 4 µm × 25 µm and the single-photon electronic temperature excursion is of order 2 K. This is the closest current photon-sensitive transducer benchmark.

Collision implication: `photon -> hot electrons -> Josephson escape` is prior art. Any Experiment-03 contribution must reside elsewhere: persistent fluxoid capture, directional/self-biased mapping, a new theorem/bound, or a quantitatively distinct operating regime.

## C. Superconducting photogalvanic / phase-battery theory

### Mironov, Mel'nikov, and Buzdin, Phys. Rev. B 109, L220503 (2024)

**Title:** Photogalvanic phenomena in superconductors supporting intrinsic diode effect

Relevant result: phenomenological theory predicts an electromagnetic wave can generate a superconducting phase difference in a material/structure with intrinsic diode effect. Closing it into a superconducting loop generates a dc circulating supercurrent; increasing illumination can switch loop states with different vorticities.

Collision implication: `illumination -> superconducting phase battery -> circulating loop current/vorticity switching` is already explicit prior art. Experiment 03 cannot claim that broad chain as new. The unresolved question is whether **single absorbed LWIR photons** can be converted with high fidelity into persistent directionally selected flux states under a stochastic calorimetric/phase-slip architecture and whether a new bound/optimization law emerges.

## D. Field-free Josephson/superconducting diode directionality

### Wu et al., Nature 604, 653–656 (2022)

**Title:** The field-free Josephson diode in a van der Waals heterostructure  
**Article:** s41586-022-04504-8

Relevant result: NbSe2/Nb3Br8/NbSe2 heterostructures exhibit field-free nonreciprocal Josephson critical currents / superconducting diode behavior due to inversion-symmetry breaking.

Collision implication: field-free superconducting directionality is prior art. Experiment 03 must not claim the Josephson diode effect itself.

### Bauriedl et al., Nature Communications 13, 4266 (2022)

**Title:** Supercurrent diode effect and magnetochiral anisotropy in few-layer NbSe2  
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-022-31954-5

Relevant result: substantial supercurrent rectification was demonstrated in few-layer NbSe2 under symmetry-breaking conditions. Useful for understanding achievable nonreciprocity and possible directional phase-escape landscapes.

## Current literature verdict

The ingredients are individually crowded prior art. The broad idea "light creates superconducting phase/current" is not novel. The research branch remains justified only because the exact conjunction

```text
single LWIR photon
-> transient photon-sensitive Josephson perturbation
-> directionally selected fluxoid transition
-> persistent superconducting memory output
```

plus any rigorous dark-count/efficiency closure has not yet been collision-audited. No priority claim is authorized.
