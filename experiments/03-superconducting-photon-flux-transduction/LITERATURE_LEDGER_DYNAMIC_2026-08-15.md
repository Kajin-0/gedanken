## 1. Dissipative quantum tunneling

### Caldeira and Leggett, Physical Review Letters 46, 211 (1981)

**Title:** Influence of Dissipation on Quantum Tunneling in Macroscopic Systems  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.46.211

A metastable quantum degree of freedom linearly coupled to a dissipative environment acquires a modified tunneling probability. Linear dissipation is therefore part of the quantum escape problem, not merely a classical damping correction.

**Experiment-03 implication:** the scalar-R deterministic capture model and the provisional cubic MQT diagnostic cannot remain independent physical knobs in the final theory.

### Ambegaokar, Eckern, and Schoen, Physical Review Letters 48, 1745 (1982)

**Title:** Quantum Dynamics of Tunneling between Superconductors  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.48.1745

A functional-integral treatment of microscopic Josephson tunneling includes dissipative quasiparticle effects and connects Josephson phase dynamics to the quantum-Brownian-motion environment framework.

**Experiment-03 implication:** a final cold dark-count calculation should use the same physical environment that controls real-time phase damping and fluctuations.

## 2. Frequency-dependent environment and retrapping

### Männik et al., Physical Review B 71, 220509(R) (2005)

**Title:** Crossover from Kramers to phase-diffusion switching in moderately damped Josephson junctions  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevB.71.220509

The measured switching rates of a hysteretic dc SQUID are modified by phase retrapping, and the retrapping process is affected by the frequency-dependent environmental impedance.

**Collision/physics implication:** Experiment 03 must not present frequency-dependent impedance affecting capture/retrapping as a new general Josephson phenomenon. Its relevance is detector-specific.

## 3. Stochastic graphene-Josephson phase dynamics

### Guarcello et al., Physical Review B 92, 174519 (2015)

**Title:** Phase dynamics in graphene-based Josephson junctions in the presence of thermal and correlated fluctuations  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevB.92.174519

Thermal and colored noise were studied in driven graphene-based Josephson junction phase escape, including switching-time distributions, damping dependence, stochastic resonant activation and noise-enhanced stability.

**Collision implication:** generic noisy graphene-JJ metastable switching and switching-time statistics are prior art.

## 4. Quantum/classical capture into Josephson basins

### Pashin, Satanin, and Kim, Physical Review E 99, 062223 (2019)

**Title:** Classical and quantum dissipative dynamics in Josephson junctions: An Arnold problem, bifurcation, and capture into resonance  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevE.99.062223

The work studies dissipative Josephson phase dynamics near bistability and explicitly asks for the probability of capture into either basin of attraction in both classical and quantum descriptions.

**Major collision:** neither `quantum basin capture probability` nor the generic idea of optimizing Josephson-basin capture is new to Experiment 03.

The surviving distinction must come from the specific detector chain and/or a new closure:

```text
single absorbed LWIR photon
-> time-dependent proximity-JJ CPR/potential
-> nonadiabatic finite-time basin map
-> persistent superconducting flux state
+ simultaneous dark/rise/environment constraints.
```

## 5. Recent stochastic phase measurements

### Haxell et al., Physical Review Letters 130, 087002 (2023)

**Title:** Measurements of Phase Dynamics in Planar Josephson Junctions and SQUIDs  
**DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.087002

Planar Josephson junctions and SQUIDs show experimentally measurable stochastic phase dynamics with a crossover between macroscopic quantum tunneling and phase diffusion; damping/capacitance materially affect switching distributions.

**Experiment-03 implication:** a final model needs realistic stochastic phase physics rather than deterministic basin boundaries plus an independent dark-rate formula.
